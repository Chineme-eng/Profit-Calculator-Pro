What actually moves your margin
01
Cut your biggest cost first
Shaving 5% off your largest line item does more than eliminating ten small ones. Always attack the tallest bar first.
02
Revenue ≠ profit
A $50k month at 8% margin earns $4k. A $10k month at 40% earns $4k. Same money, very different businesses.
03
Digital scales, physical doesn't
Digital products and SaaS can reach 60–80% margins. Physical products hit a cost ceiling that scales slowly.
04
Ad spend is a variable cost
Every dollar in ad spend should return more than a dollar in margin. If your ROAS drops below break-even ROAS, you're paying to lose money.
05
Model your tax from day one
Self-employment tax (15.3% US), income tax, and sales tax all come off profit. Use the tax slider — it shows what you actually keep.
06
Retainers beat one-off sales
One client paying $1k/month is worth more than twelve clients paying $1k once. Recurring revenue means lower acquisition cost per dollar.
07
Know your hourly rate always
Divide your monthly profit by hours worked. If it's below minimum wage, the business model needs to change, not your work ethic.
08
Sensitivity before scaling
Before hiring or increasing ad spend, stress test your model. What happens if revenue drops 20%? Use the sensitivity table to find out.
